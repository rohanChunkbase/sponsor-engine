`quote_booking` calls `quotePrice` in `packages/pricing`.
The currency is GBP.
GBP means pounds sterling.
The package reads the past bookings of one venue.
A community pays less than a company.
A community can pay nothing.

<Warning>WARNING: Do not use the SAMPLE prices for a real venue. The file data/sample-bookings.csv is not real. The venue name Riverworks Hall is not real.</Warning>

## What you get

The quote returns these fields.

- `price`: the room quote in GBP
- `currency`: the text `GBP`
- `range`: `low` and `high`, in GBP
- `explanation`: plain text for a person
- `comparables`: the past bookings that support the quote

The explanation tells you the community price and the company price.
The explanation names the past bookings that support the price.
Food is not inside the room price.

## Food is a separate ask

The food rate is £5.50 for each person the forecast expects.
`draft_sponsor_ask` asks for that food cost.
The sponsor email does not add a room fee.

The forecast uses the community history when that community has at least three events with a show-up rate.
When that community has fewer than three events, the forecast uses other bookings of the same kind when three or more exist.
When fewer than three bookings of that kind exist, a free community event uses a 28% no-show.
Show-up is then 72%.
A company with no history uses an 88% show-up until three company bookings exist.

An 80-person community evening on the benchmark path expects 58 people.
The food ask is then £319.
Your history can change this number.

## Two methods

The package fits two methods on the past bookings.
The package scores both methods on held-out rows.
The package uses the method with the lower error.

kNN means k nearest neighbors.
Here k is 8.
The nearest-booking method finds the 8 nearest past bookings with the same booker type.
The method takes a weighted median of those prices.
A nearer booking has a higher weight.
When fewer than 3 bookings of that type exist, the method uses the nearest bookings of any type.

The free-slot rule can set the price to £0.
All of these must be true.

- The booker is a community.
- The booker brings leads.
- At least half of those nearest bookings were free slots.

The method rounds the price to the nearest £5.

The second method is a gradient boosted model.
The model adds many small decision trees.
The trees learn the logarithm of (1 + price).
The quote converts the tree result back to GBP.
The quote rounds that price to the nearest £5.
On the SAMPLE fit, the model uses 52 trees.
Each new tree uses a step size of 0.08.

## Held-out accuracy

MAE means mean absolute error.
MAE is the mean of the absolute price errors, in GBP.
MAPE means mean absolute percentage error.
MAPE uses only rows with a price above £0.
A free slot has no percentage.

The split sorts the bookings by date, then by booking id.
The fit uses the first 80 percent of the rows.
The score uses the last 20 percent of the rows.
The SAMPLE score uses 240 rows for the fit and 60 rows for the score.
The quote for a new request fits the winner on every row in the history.
The MAE does not use that full fit.
The MAE uses the held-out split only.

The winner is the method with the lower MAE.
When the MAE values are equal, the winner is the method with the lower MAPE.
When those values are also equal, the winner is the nearest-booking method.
A constant median is a baseline.
The median cannot win.

`npm run evaluate` in `packages/pricing` prints the SAMPLE scores.

| Method | MAE | MAPE on 37 paid rows | Free slots at £0 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Nearest bookings (winner) | £76.92 | 15.9 percent | 22 of 23 |
| Gradient boosted trees | £96.75 | 13.0 percent | 22 of 23 |
| Median baseline | £721.83 | 70.6 percent | 0 of 23 |

The nearest-booking method wins on MAE.
The gradient boosted model has the lower MAPE.
The quote uses the nearest-booking method.

The dashboard Accuracy card calculates this error on the active history.
The numbers are not typed in.
Drop a venue CSV on the Quote page to run the check again.

## Your venue file

The history must contain at least 10 bookings.
One file is for one venue.
The loader accepts the columns in `packages/pricing/README.md`.
The app stores a dropped CSV at `data/venue-bookings.csv`.
That file replaces the SAMPLE history for the next quote.

## Limits

The model can only repeat patterns that are in the history.
Do not send a booker type that is not in the column list.
The column list is `community`, `startup`, `company`, and `agency`.
MAPE does not include a price of £0.
The explanation rounds MAE to the nearest pound.
The evaluation script prints MAE with two decimal places.
