Sponsor Engine ships two data packs.
One pack is public London events.
One pack is fictional.
Read the label on a row before you repeat a name.

## Real London tables

`data/real-data/` holds public London events and sponsors.
The tables were collected on 22 October 2026 from public pages.
Each claim has a short quote and a source URL.

The pack includes these files.

- 61 London tech events, from 2 October 2025 to 10 November 2026
- 201 sponsor claims, one per event, company, and role
- 130 companies
- 42 communities
- 61 community-to-community links

A claim confidence of `stated` means the page says it.
A claim confidence of `inferred` means the table records a reading of the page.
The source URL is on the row.

The tables hold no personal email address.
The tables hold no phone number.
The tables hold no attendee list.
Raw HTML was removed because it contained guest names.
`raw/luma_events.json` keeps public event fields.
Host names and guest names were removed from that file.

Luma pages were fetched at one request every two seconds.
The Luma robots file allows event pages.
Meetup, AI Tinkerers, and other public pages were read with a fetch.
The quote on each claim is a short line from that page.

Food payers are rarely named.
Most food rows are a tech company that paid for food.
One caterer in the pack is Swift Food.

<Warning>WARNING: Do not treat a short quote as a right to copy the whole page. Open the source URL. Follow the licence on that site.</Warning>

## Fictional tables

`data/synthetic-data/` is fictional.
Communities are marked SAMPLE.
Companies and people are marked fictional.
Emails use `example.com` or `example.org`.

<Warning>WARNING: Do not present the fictional pack as real companies, real people, or real events.</Warning>

The fictional pack includes communities, events, sponsors, deals, members, and a sample organiser history.
The sample organiser show-up across 10 events is 62.1 percent.
That number belongs to the fictional persona.
It is not a London census figure.

## Venue prices

`packages/pricing/data/sample-bookings.csv` is a SAMPLE price file.
The venue name is Riverworks Hall.
Riverworks Hall is not a real venue.
Every price in that file is generated.
The seed is `20261022`.
The file has 300 rows.

<Warning>WARNING: Do not use the SAMPLE prices for a real venue.</Warning>

A dropped venue CSV replaces that file for quotes.
Store a redacted export.
Redacted means the venue removed private names before the export.
One file is for one venue.
